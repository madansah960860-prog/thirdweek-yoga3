Weekly email

The Slow Start

A slower way into the week: one short practice and one plainly written note, sent on Monday morning and typed out in full inside the email, so you never have to click anything to use it. Free. No account needed to read a word of this site, and one click takes you off the list for good.

The format

Two things, every week

One email lands on Monday. It contains exactly two items, in the same order, every time.

  • A practice, five to twenty minutes, written out in the email with hold times against each pose. Nothing is hidden behind a link.
  • A note, usually three or four paragraphs on one specific thing: a cue that changes a pose, why a prop sits where it sits, or an instruction that gets read the wrong way round.

That is the whole shape and it does not drift. No round-ups, no affiliate blasts, no product launch dressed as a note. If a week's email carries a paid placement, it is labelled as a paid placement inside the email, where you will read it.

Things that never arrive

  • Daily sends. One per week, on Monday, and no extras.
  • Health, medical or therapeutic claims, in the email or anywhere else on this site.
  • Weight loss, calorie counts, body transformation or diet content of any kind.
  • Countdown offers, fake deadlines or pressure sequences.

Monday, 06:00 Pacific

What actually lands in the inbox

This is a real issue taken apart, top to bottom, so you can decide before you hand over an address rather than after. The whole thing reads in about four minutes.

  1. Subject line: "Ten minutes, and a word about your back foot"

    The subject says what is inside. No teasers, no fake urgency, no re-sends to people who did not open the first one.

  2. The practice, written out

    Between five and twenty minutes. Every pose named, every hold given in seconds, every prop option listed. A sample week: Cat and Cow for 60 seconds, low lunge for 45 per side, Downward-Facing Dog for 30, Tree for 30 per side, Child's Pose for 90. Links to the full pose guides sit at the end, for anyone who wants them.

  3. The note, three or four paragraphs

    One idea, plainly explained. Why the back foot turns in rather than out. What the block under the hip is doing in a seated twist. Why the second side of a pose often feels better than the first.

  4. The footer

    Who sent it, the postal address, and the one-click unsubscribe link. Same three items, same place, every week.

  • Sent Monday morning, US Pacific time, once a week and never more.
  • Length Around four minutes to read, with the practice ready to use as it stands.
  • Price Free, and it stays free whether or not you ever take a membership.
  • Leaving One click in the footer, effective immediately, with no survey in the way.

The newsletter is a convenience, not a gate. Every word of this site is readable without it.

Plain terms

How we handle your address

Some of this is written out because the law requires it. The rest is written out because you should be able to read it before you type an address in, rather than afterwards.

  • Who is sending it. Every email comes from Stillform, from the address hello@stillform.co. The sender name and the subject line will always describe accurately what is inside.
  • Our physical mailing address appears in the footer of every single email: Stillform, 812 Alder Street, Studio 4, Portland, OR 97205, United States.
  • Unsubscribing. Every email carries a working one-click unsubscribe link in the footer. It takes effect immediately, with no login, no survey, and no step that asks whether you are sure. You can also reply to any email with the word unsubscribe, or write to hello@stillform.co and we will remove you by hand.
  • What we store. Your email address, your first name if you gave one, your practice-length answer if you gave one, and the dates you opened or clicked. Nothing else.
  • We do not sell or rent your address, to anyone, for any purpose. Where a sponsored placement runs in an email, the sponsor receives aggregate numbers only and never your address.
  • How long we keep it. Until you unsubscribe, plus thirty days, after which the record is deleted. If you have not opened an email in eighteen months we take you off the list automatically.

The full detail sits in the Privacy Policy. If you are in California, the CCPA and CPRA section sets out your rights and exactly how to exercise them.
