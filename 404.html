Looking for one pose

If you came here after a pose name

Pose pages were renamed when the site was rebuilt, so an older link is likely to land here. These six carry the full written breakdown: entry steps, alignment cues, a breath count, a hold time, the mistakes people make, and at least two prop setups.

Downward-Facing Dog

Adho Mukha Svanasana. Where the heels really go, and why bent knees are the standard version.

Warrior II

Virabhadrasana II. Back foot, front knee, and the two things that make the hip ache.

Tree Pose

Vrksasana. Balance beside a wall with two fingertips, which is where everybody should start.

Bridge Pose

Setu Bandha Sarvangasana. A block under the sacrum turns this into a resting pose.

Child's Pose

Balasana. Three setups for knees, hips and head, none of them a compromise.

Seated Forward Fold

Paschimottanasana. Sit on a folded blanket first. It changes the whole shape.

Stillform, on corrections

Email hello@stillform.co and tell us which page you were on when you clicked, or use the contact form and pick the correction topic. You will hear back within two business days, and broken links get repaired in the same week they are reported.

How this site is funded

Nothing on Stillform sits behind a login or a paywall, and nothing that is free today gets moved behind one later. The writing is paid for four ways: labeled display advertising placed below the content, an optional $6 membership that removes those ads, commissions on the props we recommend, and paid downloads for people who want the printable version. Sponsored pieces say sponsored in the first line, above the headline. Affiliate links carry their disclosure at the link itself, not buried down here. Read the full disclosure.
