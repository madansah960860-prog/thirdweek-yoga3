02 / The limits

Written down on purpose

What membership deliberately does not do

Pricing pages usually list what you gain. This is the other list, set out so that nobody pays $6 expecting one of these.

  • It opens no instruction Every pose guide, sequence and article stays readable without an account, permanently.
  • It is not a class subscription There is no video library, no live schedule and no teacher on the other end of it.
  • It buys no teaching time Members get no personal feedback, no plan written for them and no correspondence course.
  • It removes no affiliate links Those sit inside written recommendations and stay disclosed at the point of use.
  • It removes no sponsored articles Those stay too, still labelled as sponsored at the top of the page.
  • It changes no editorial decision Members do not vote on what gets written, and neither do advertisers.

If the price is a genuine obstacle, write to hello@stillform.co and ask. There is no form and no proof required.

Six revenue sources

Where the money comes from

Six sources, all of them disclosed on this page. There is no seventh.

  • Display advertising. Every page except the legal pages and the 404 page carries one labelled ad slot, placed below the main content. Ads never sit above the content in the fold, never touch a button or the navigation, and the space is reserved in the layout so nothing moves when an ad loads. Advertising cookies are not set until you consent to them.
  • Membership. $6 per month or $54 per year in USD, described above.
  • Affiliate commissions on mats, blocks, straps, blankets and bolsters. If you buy through one of our links we earn a commission at no additional cost to you. Every affiliate link carries its disclosure right at the link rather than only down in the footer, and every prop we recommend has a free household substitute listed next to it.
  • Paid downloads, the three products listed above.
  • Sponsored content and brand partnerships. An article paid for by a brand is labelled as sponsored at the top of the page, above the first paragraph. We do not publish sponsored content that makes health claims, and we do not take sponsorship from supplement, diet, cleanse or weight-loss businesses.
  • The teacher directory. A basic listing is free. A featured placement is paid, and is marked as featured everywhere it appears. Details here.

The complete version, including the FTC-required affiliate language, is on the Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure page.
