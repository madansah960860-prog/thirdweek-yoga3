01 / Before you send

Four lines of detail

What to include so we can actually help

Most of the messages that take three emails to sort out could have taken one. These are the details that make the difference, and they take about a minute to gather.

  • The page Paste the address from your browser bar, or name the pose or sequence.
  • The line Quote the sentence that is wrong, unclear or broken.
  • Your setup Phone or laptop, which browser, and whether you use a screen reader.
  • What you expected One sentence on what you thought would happen instead.

If it is a correction

Tell us the page, the sentence and what is wrong with it. A source helps but is not required; a plain "the Sanskrit here is misspelled" is enough to act on. We check it, fix it, and add a dated note at the foot of the page saying what changed.

If something will not work

Tell us the page, your device and browser, and what you were trying to do. Say too if you use a screen reader, keyboard navigation, zoom above 200 percent or reduced motion. That one detail matters more than anything else on the list, because it tells us where to start looking.

If it is about billing

Give us the email address on the membership and the last four digits of the card. Do not send us the full card number, and we will never ask you for it. Cancellation does not need an email at all: it is two clicks on your account page.

If you are writing about a listing

Tell us the business name and the city and state exactly as they should appear. If you are asking us to change a live listing, say what the entry says now and what it should say instead.

02 / Timing

How long things take

When you will hear back

These are the windows we hold ourselves to, in business days, Monday to Friday, Pacific Time. If something slips past one of them, write again and say so; that is not a nuisance, it is useful.

  • First reply, any message: two business days. A real answer where we have one, and an honest "we are looking into it" where we do not.
  • A correction on a page: five business days from the point we agree it is wrong, with a dated note recording the change.
  • An accessibility report: two business days to reply, ten to fix or to publish a plan if the fix is larger than that. Every report is logged either way.
  • Billing and refunds: two business days. Refunds inside the first 30 days are processed without a conversation about why.
  • Privacy and data requests: 45 calendar days, which is the CCPA window, though most are answered inside a week.
  • Directory submissions: two business days to confirm the details with you before anything is published.
  • Partnership enquiries: two business days, including the ones we turn down.

Phone lines run Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Pacific Time. There is no queue and no menu. If nobody picks up, leave a number and you will get a call back the same day.

Scope

What we can and cannot answer

We can answer: how a pose guide is put together, which sequence fits a situation you describe, how membership or the directory works, corrections, anything on the site that is getting in your way, and every question about what happens to your data.

We cannot answer: anything about a health condition, an injury, a medication, a pregnancy, or whether a given pose is safe for you specifically. Those belong with a professional who can examine you. We will say so rather than guess, and that is not us being unhelpful; it is the only honest answer a website has.

We cannot teach you by email. Written instruction has a ceiling, and above it the useful thing is a person in the room. The directory is a place to start, and it carries a list of questions worth asking before you book.

We would rather send you a short answer today than a confident one that turns out to be wrong.

How this site is funded

Nothing on Stillform sits behind a login or a paywall, and nothing that is free today gets moved behind one later. The writing is paid for four ways: labeled display advertising placed below the content, an optional $6 membership that removes those ads, commissions on the props we recommend, and paid downloads for people who want the printable version. Sponsored pieces say sponsored in the first line, above the headline. Affiliate links carry their disclosure at the link itself, not buried down here. Read the full disclosure.
