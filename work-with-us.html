Partnerships

Work with us

Display advertising, sponsored articles, affiliate partnerships and featured directory placement. What you can buy, how it gets labeled, what we decline, and how long each step takes.

The short version, before you write to us

Everything paid is labeled. Sponsored articles say so above the first paragraph. Affiliate links carry a disclosure beside the link. Featured directory listings are marked as featured. We take no work from supplement, diet, cleanse, fasting or weight-loss businesses, and we publish nothing that says yoga treats a health condition. If that rules out the campaign, we are the wrong site, and we would rather you knew that in paragraph one than in week three.

  • Reply time Two business days to every enquiry, including the ones we turn down.
  • Lead time Ten business days from a signed brief to a sponsored piece going live.
  • Rates Quoted in writing, per campaign, before any work starts. Ask and we send the card.

Ad placement

Display advertising

Every page except the legal pages and the 404 page carries one labeled display slot, sitting below the content of the page.

  • Where it goes. Under the main content block. Never above the fold, never beside navigation, never touching a button, never dropped into the middle of an instructional sequence.
  • How many. One slot per page. Content outweighs advertising on every page of this site, and that ratio is not up for negotiation on a per-campaign basis.
  • Layout. The slot holds a reserved height at every breakpoint, so nothing shifts under a reader's thumb when the ad arrives.
  • Labeling. A visible "Advertisement" label sits above every slot, and the slot is styled to look different from the writing around it.
  • Consent. No advertising cookie is set until a visitor agrees through the banner. Visitors who decline non-essential cookies see non-personalized advertising only.
  • What we will not run. Pop-ups, pop-unders, entry interstitials, auto-playing audio or video, auto-redirects, auto-downloads, and anything that interferes with the back button.

Display inventory is sold through our ad partners rather than direct. For a direct placement, use the form further down.

Sponsored work

Sponsored articles

We run a small number each year. The terms are the same for all of them and none of them move.

  • Labeled at the top. The sponsor is named above the first paragraph. Not at the bottom, not in a footer, not in grey six-point type.
  • Written here, or edited here. Copy that arrives from a sponsor is edited to the standards the rest of the site is held to, which includes cutting any health claim in it.
  • No health claims, anywhere. A sponsored piece cannot say or imply that a product, pose, sequence or practice treats, cures, heals, relieves or prevents anything. That applies to the sponsor's own product copy as well.
  • No weight-loss framing. No calorie counts, no fat burning, no body transformation, no before-and-after, no toning.
  • No fake urgency. No countdown clocks, no invented scarcity, no discount that expires at midnight.
  • Editorial control stays here. Sponsors read the piece before it publishes and can correct a fact about their own product. They do not sign off the framing, the conclusion, or anything else on the page.

You can buy space on this site. You cannot buy a sentence that says yoga will fix someone's back.

01 / The filter

Before the call

What we will and will not publish

Most enquiries can be answered from this list alone, which saves everybody a meeting. Read down the right-hand column first. If your product is in it, the answer is no, and no rewrite of the brief changes that.

Yes, with labeling

  • Mats, blocks, straps, blankets, bolsters and other physical practice equipment.
  • Studios, teacher trainings and in-person workshops operating in the United States.
  • Apps and software for practice timing, scheduling or class booking.
  • Clothing and footwear, on plain product terms, described as clothing rather than as performance.
  • Books, printed courses and anything else made of writing.
  • Props brands that will let us name a free household substitute in the same paragraph.

No, whatever the budget

  • Supplements, powders, teas, tinctures and anything else ingested.
  • Diet programs, cleanses, detoxes, fasting protocols and weight-loss products or services.
  • Anything sold on a health outcome: pain relief, blood pressure, fertility, immunity, sleep as a medical claim.
  • Medical devices, wearables marketed on diagnosis, and posture correctors.
  • Credential mills, unaccredited certification schemes, and anything sold on recruiting other sellers.
  • Link insertions into existing articles, paid guest posts and any request for an undisclosed do-follow link.

Two smaller rules that catch people out. We do not publish "best of" roundups built to carry affiliate links, so a product cannot buy its way into a comparison. And we do not backdate, quietly edit or unlabel an old sponsored piece after the campaign ends: it stays up, and it stays labeled.

02 / How it runs

Rates and process

What it costs and how long it takes

Rates are quoted in writing, per campaign, before anything begins, and the quote holds for 30 days. There is no minimum spend on a first placement and no annual commitment on any product. We do not price on performance, because performance pricing gives a publisher a reason to write the wrong article.

  1. Day one: you write, we answer

    Send the form below or email hello@stillform.co. Within two business days you get a yes, a no, or a question. The no comes with the reason attached, because a vague decline wastes your next month.

  2. Day two to three: the quote

    You get a written quote covering the format, the placement, the dates, the labeling that will appear and the deliverables. One page, plain figures, no tiers with names like gold.

  3. Day three to eight: drafting

    For a sponsored article, we write it or we edit what you send. You supply product facts and any claims substantiation. We supply the yoga, the structure and the final wording.

  4. Day eight to ten: your factual review

    One round, three business days, limited to facts about your own product: specifications, price, availability, spelling of your name. Framing and conclusions are not in scope, and that is stated in the quote before you agree to it.

  5. Publication and invoice

    The piece goes live with the sponsor label above the first paragraph. We invoice on publication, net 30, in US dollars. A placement that does not run is not invoiced.

  6. Afterward: what you get back

    A reporting note 30 days after publication with page views and read-through from our analytics provider. No dashboard access, no pixel on the page, and no visitor data leaves this site.

Display advertising, an optional $6 monthly membership that removes those ads, affiliate commissions on practice equipment, paid digital downloads, labeled sponsored articles, and paid featured placement in the teacher directory. Affiliate partnerships

We carry affiliate links on physical practice equipment: mats, blocks, straps, blankets and bolsters. Nothing else qualifies.

  • Every affiliate link carries its disclosure in the same block of content as the link, not only in the footer.
  • Every prop we recommend has a free household substitute named in the same paragraph or list item. A hardback book is a block. A bathrobe belt is a strap.
  • Commission rates decide nothing: not what appears, not what comes first, not how a product gets described.
  • No affiliate links on anything ingested, anything sold on a health outcome, or clothing.
  • No listicles built around affiliate revenue and no roundups that exist only to hold links.

The full FTC-compliant statement sits on the Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure page.

Featured directory placement

A basic listing in the teacher directory is free, permanently, for any teacher or studio working in the United States. Featured placement is paid. It sits above the basic listings for its metro area, carries a photograph and a longer description, and shows a visible "Featured placement" label everywhere it appears.

What it buys is position and room to write. It does not buy an endorsement, a review or a rating, because we publish ratings for nobody. No listing implies that Stillform has checked the teacher, and the directory page says exactly that, at the top, before the first entry.

Who reads this site

Mostly people in the United States, and mostly people in their first year of practice: someone looking up a pose by name, someone building a practice at home, someone who wants to read an instruction rather than pause a video to catch it.

The busiest pages are the beginner's guide, the pose library, the four sequences and the props guide. Most arrivals come from search, on queries about a named pose or a specific length of practice.

We do not print visitor numbers here. A figure on this page would be stale within a month and unverifiable by you in any case. Current numbers, straight from our analytics provider, go to prospective partners on request.

The constraint

Why we hold the line here

Yoga sits next to a very large market in health claims, most of it unsupported. Writing that a pose eases back pain, that a breathing pattern lowers blood pressure, or that a sequence burns a set number of calories would be easy, and every one of those pages would do well.

We do not, for two reasons. Those claims are not ours to make: this site is written by teachers, not clinicians. And a site that makes them stops being believable about everything else it says, including the things it genuinely knows, like where the back foot goes in Warrior II.

Partners who find that constraint reasonable tend to fit well here. Partners who do not find out inside forty-eight hours, which is better for both of us than finding out in month two.
