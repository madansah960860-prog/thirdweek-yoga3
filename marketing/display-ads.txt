COMPLIANCE NOTES FOR THIS SET
------------------------------------------------------------------------
No all-caps words. No repeated or excessive punctuation. No emoji or gimmicky
symbols. No reference to the ad itself. No superlatives and no unsubstantiated
claims. No fake urgency, scarcity or countdowns. No health, medical or
therapeutic claims. No weight-loss, calorie-burn, slimming or body
transformation language. No competitor, studio, app or trademarked brand names.
No certification, lineage or accreditation claims. No phone numbers. Every line
is written to stand alone, because Google mixes assets automatically. Angles
vary across the set: beginner starting points, short practices for busy
schedules, morning and evening routines, habit building, desk and chair work,
practice at home without equipment, and gentle or supported options.
